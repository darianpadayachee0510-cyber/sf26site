A New Era of Ferrari Engineering

The SF-26 is Ferrari’s response to the 2026 Formula 1 regulation overhaul — a machine built around efficiency, aerodynamics, and energy recovery. Every surface is designed to control airflow and extract maximum performance from a new generation of hybrid power.

Aerodynamic Philosophy

Ferrari has focused on a low-drag, high-efficiency concept. The SF-26 features refined sidepod inlets, aggressive floor shaping, and a cleaner rear end to maximize airflow toward the diffuser.

The “Macarena” Wing

One of the most talked-about innovations is Ferrari’s flexible rear wing concept, often referred to as the “Macarena wing.” Designed to subtly adjust under load, it reduces drag on straights while maintaining downforce in corners — giving Ferrari a crucial edge in top speed without sacrificing grip.

Hybrid Power Unit

The 2026 regulations place greater emphasis on electrical power. Ferrari’s new hybrid system delivers increased energy deployment, improved battery efficiency, and better energy recovery under braking.

Chassis & Weight Design

The SF-26 chassis is built using ultra-light carbon fibre construction to maximize strength while reducing overall weight. Engineers have focused on improving rigidity, crash safety, and weight distribution to enhance cornering performance.

Every gram matters in modern Formula 1 — the SF-26 is designed to stay as close to the minimum weight limit as possible while maintaining structural integrity under extreme loads.
